About
Quranic

Quranic is a solo-developed web application built to provide a clean, distraction-free reading experience of the Holy Quran.

Every feature — surah browsing, translation, search, bookmarks, and daily ayahs — is built and maintained by a single developer with the goal of making the Quran accessible to everyone.

No ads. No clutter. Just the Quran.
